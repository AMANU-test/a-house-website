Tier 01 — Live

Management Board

The six department Chiefs who make up A-HOUSE's C-suite, each reporting directly to the CEO and owning one department end to end.

Tier 02 — Live

Executive Board

Heads run each department day-to-day and report directly to their Chief to operationalize the hub's overarching strategy.

Tier 03 — Live

Department Teams

Leads run one team's slice of the work under a Head, and report directly to them.
