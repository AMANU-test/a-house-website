About Us

Building Ateneo's
entrepreneurial ecosystem.

We, the members of A-HOUSE, are united by our passion for entrepreneurship and startups, and guided by the principles of integrity, innovation, and community service.

What drives us

Vision & Mission

VISION

To create a dynamic and inclusive entrepreneurial ecosystem where every student can innovate, build startups, and make a lasting positive impact on society.

MISSION

To democratize the process of building startups by providing students with access to industry experts, mentorship, and opportunities within and beyond the Ateneo de Manila University, fostering a culture of innovation, entrepreneurship, and social responsibility.

Core Competencies

Not just A-HOUSE — also a HOME

01 · HARMONY

Welcoming all students, regardless of their background or major, to access resources and support for their entrepreneurial endeavors.

02 · OPPORTUNITY

Providing students with opportunities to connect with industry experts and engage with the broader entrepreneurial ecosystem outside the university — including internships, competitions, and partnerships.

03 · MENTORSHIP

Offering practical learning experiences through workshops, seminars, and real-world projects, while empowering students to spread entrepreneurial knowledge and opportunities to underserved communities.

04 · ENGAGEMENT

Actively engaging students in networking opportunities with entrepreneurs, investors, and professionals to foster collaboration and idea exchange, while committing to the continuous improvement of our programs and resources.

Membership

Who can join

All enrolled undergraduate or exchange students of the Loyola Schools of the Ateneo de Manila University are eligible for membership in A-HOUSE.

Full membership

Complete the Member Application Form, attend the First General Assembly, and pay the Registration Fee — or be a retained member from the previous year.

Rights as a member

Voice feedback and ideas, run for office, propose Constitutional amendments, and be informed of every organization meeting and activity.

Retention

Members contribute to at least two A-HOUSE projects a year — as an officer, project head, core team member, or event attendee.
